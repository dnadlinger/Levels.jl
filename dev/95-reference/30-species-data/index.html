Atomic data

Level structure data for the supported species — ⁸⁸Sr⁺ (sr88) and ⁴³Ca⁺ (ca43) — with literature citations for every value.

Levels.ca43 — Constant

⁴³Ca⁺ ion (nuclear spin $I = 7/2$).

Hyperfine centroids are referenced to the S$_{1/2}$ centroid; all hyperfine $A$/$B$ constants are entered as stated in the cited measurements (the signs follow from $μ_I < 0$). The electronic g-factors and Einstein A coefficients marked as such are ⁴⁰Ca⁺ measurements; their isotope dependence is far below the quoted uncertainties.
