Optics utilities

Gaussian beam geometry and the intensity distribution that goes with it, used to turn an optical power into the intensity seen by an ion.

These functions are generic over the element type, so they work with symbolic expressions as well as unitful quantities.

Levels.GaussBeamParams — Type

Parameters of a Gaussian beam propagating along the $z$ axis.

Generic over the type; the code supports both unitful quantities and symbolic expressions.

source
Levels.gauss_intensity — Method
gauss_intensity(power, waist, wavelength; r_offset, z_offset)

Returns the intensity at the given point of a Gaussian beam with the specified parameters and total power.

Arguments

  • power: The optical power integrated over the whole beam profile.
  • waist: The $1/e^2$-radius of the beam at its waist.
  • wavelength: The wavelength of the radiation.
  • r_offset: If given, the distance of the point from the focus in the radial direction.
  • z_offset: If given, the distance of the point from the focus along the direction of propagation.
source
Levels.gauss_intensity — Method
gauss_intensity(power, beam::GaussBeamParams; r_offset, z_pos)

Returns the intensity at the given point of the given Gaussian beam with the specified total power.

Arguments

  • power: The optical power integrated over the whole beam profile.
  • beam: The beam parameters, including the position of the waist along the direction of propagation.
  • r_offset: If given, the distance of the point from the beam axis. Defaults to on-axis.
  • z_pos: If given, the position of the point along the direction of propagation, in the same frame of reference as beam.waist_pos. Defaults to the waist.
