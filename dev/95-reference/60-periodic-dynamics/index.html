Periodic dynamics

Levels.PeriodicDynamics — Module

Periodic driving of laser-probed atomic transitions: ac Zeeman coupling, micromotion phase modulation, sideband Rabi frequencies and resonance shifts.

An Eigenstructure supplies the static atom. DrivenTransition adds the probe laser and harmonic drives in the optical rotating-wave approximation. Its matrices use the structure's field eigenbasis, including field-induced hyperfine mixing.

sideband_rabi offers two methods: DressedStates dresses the manifolds with the periodic drive and treats the laser to first order; Monodromy propagates the full model and finds an avoided-crossing gap. quasienergy_shift evaluates laser-free shifts with PerturbationTheory or nonperturbative Floquet dressing.

Cf. Joshi et al., "Characterization of ion-trap-induced ac magnetic fields", Phys. Rev. A 110, 063101 (2024), doi:10.1103/PhysRevA.110.063101.

Driven-transition model

Levels.PeriodicDynamics.DrivenTransition — Type

Rotating-frame model of a laser-probed transition between two manifolds, in the optical rotating-wave approximation:

\[H(t) = \mathrm{diag}(\texttt{frame}) - δ P_\mathrm{upper} + \sum_i X_i \cos(Ω t + φ_i) + f(t) L + f(t)^* L^†, \qquad f(t) = e^{-iφ_\mathrm{mod}(t)}/2.\]

frame, drive amplitudes X_i, laser coupling L and detuning δ are in angular-frequency units. Detuning from the probed component and phase modulation are supplied at evaluation time through full_hamiltonian or sideband_rabi, so one model can serve a parameter scan.

DrivenTransition(s, probe; …) constructs the model in the structure's field eigenbasis. The keyword constructor DrivenTransition(; basis, drive_frequency, frame, drives, coupling, lower, upper, lower_range, upper_range) accepts manually assembled data without validation; all arrays must use the declared basis and ordering.

Levels.PeriodicDynamics.DrivenTransition — Method
DrivenTransition(s::Eigenstructure, probe::Pair; drive_frequency, coupling,
                 drives = HarmonicDrive[])

Assembles a periodic model probing the lower => upper states of s. The static frame diagonal uses Levels.offsets relative to that pair.

coupling is a complex angular Rabi-frequency matrix over basis(s), with entries only in the upper-row, lower-column block. It may come from Levels.coupling_matrix(s, …), from a relative matrix scaled with Levels.rabi_normalised, or from manually specified eigenstate couplings. A coupled-basis operator must first pass through Levels.to_eigenbasis. drives contains HarmonicDrives at drive_frequency, for example from zeeman_drives(s, b). All input matrices use the structure's field eigenbasis; the constructor performs no rotation.

The basis must consist of exactly the two complete fine-structure manifolds of the probe, each contiguous. Drives must be block-diagonal in these manifolds. Complete manifolds are required for the within-manifold Floquet dressing; see Levels.iscomplete.

Levels.PeriodicDynamics.HarmonicDrive — Type

A Hermitian drive term $X \cos(Ω t + φ)$ at the fundamental drive frequency $Ω$ of a DrivenTransition.

source
Levels.PeriodicDynamics.HarmonicPhaseModulation — Type

Sinusoidal phase modulation $φ(t) = β_\mathrm{cos} \cos(Ω t) + β_\mathrm{sin} \sin(Ω t)$ of the laser coupling of a DrivenTransition at its fundamental drive frequency.

For excess micromotion at the trap rf frequency, $β_\cos$ is the in-phase and $β_\sin$ the quadrature modulation index.

Levels.PeriodicDynamics.harmonic_block — Method

Returns the $e^{+iΩt}$ Fourier component $H_1 = Σ_i ½ e^{iφ_i} X_i$ of the given HarmonicDrives $Σ_i X_i \cos(Ω t + φ_i)$, restricted to the basis index range r, so that the drive term reads $H_1 e^{iΩt} + H_1^† e^{-iΩt}$ (the convention of dress_manifold and of the perturbative quasienergy_shift).

Levels.PeriodicDynamics.zeeman_drive — Method
zeeman_drive(s::Eigenstructure, b; phase = 0.0)

Returns the HarmonicDrive describing an ac magnetic field $\vec{b} \cos(Ω t + φ)$ with the real Cartesian amplitude vector b, via Levels.zeeman_hamiltonian(s, b): the drive matrix is expressed in the field eigenbasis of the structure, indexed by its basis, like every matrix of a DrivenTransition built on s.

source
Levels.PeriodicDynamics.zeeman_drives — Method
zeeman_drives(s::Eigenstructure, b; phase = 0.0)

Returns the HarmonicDrives describing an ac magnetic field given as a complex Cartesian amplitude phasor b, $\vec{B}(t) = \mathrm{Re}[\vec{b} \, e^{-i (Ω t + φ)}]$: one drive $\mathrm{Re}(\vec{b}) \cos(Ω t + φ)$ plus, for genuinely complex b, a second $\mathrm{Im}(\vec{b}) \cos(Ω t + φ - π/2)$ (identically zero components are dropped). A real b is a linearly polarised field; e.g. $\vec{b} = B_0/\sqrt{2} \, (1, -i, 0)$ rotates in the x–y plane. The matrices are in the field eigenbasis of s (cf. zeeman_drive).

Numerical methods

Levels.PeriodicDynamics.DressedStates — Type
DressedStates(; harmonic_cutoff=8, num_time_samples=256)

Dresses each manifold with a Floquet truncation of harmonics -K:K, then computes the laser coupling to first order on num_time_samples times per period. The cutoff and time sampling should be increased to check convergence.

source
Levels.PeriodicDynamics.Floquet — Type
Floquet(; harmonic_cutoff=8, min_overlap=0.5)

Evaluates the nonperturbative quasienergy shift from Floquet dressing on -harmonic_cutoff:harmonic_cutoff. Warns when identification of the dressed state by its bare-state overlap becomes ambiguous.

source
Levels.PeriodicDynamics.Monodromy — Type
Monodromy(; bracket_scale=8.0, estimate=DressedStates(), alg=Vern8(),
           reltol=1e-10, abstol=1e-12)

Finds the sideband resonance through one-period TDSE propagation and an avoided-crossing search. Includes the laser coupling nonperturbatively within the model. estimate supplies the search centre, alg the ODE integrator. Numerical accuracy remains controlled by the propagation tolerances and search.

Levels.PeriodicDynamics.PerturbationTheory — Type
PerturbationTheory(; warn_ratio=20)

Evaluates the second-order Floquet perturbation sum. Warns when a denominator is smaller than warn_ratio times its coupling.

Dressed-state engine

Levels.PeriodicDynamics.DressedTransition — Type

A DrivenTransition with both manifolds dressed by its harmonic drives (laser off), cached so that repeated sideband_rabi evaluations (e.g. a modulation-index scan) reuse the diagonalisation.

The quasienergies ε_lower/ε_upper and periodic Floquet modes u_lower/u_upper follow the conventions of dress_manifold.

source
Levels.PeriodicDynamics.dress — Function
dress(dt::DrivenTransition, method = DressedStates())

Dresses the two manifolds of the given model by its harmonic drives, returning a DressedTransition.

method.harmonic_cutoff sets the Floquet truncation; method.num_time_samples sets the uniform time sampling per period for the Fourier analysis of the laser coupling in sideband_rabi.

source
Levels.PeriodicDynamics.dress_manifold — Method
dress_manifold(H0::Diagonal, H1::AbstractMatrix, Ω, harmonic_cutoff, num_time_samples;
               min_overlap = 0.0) -> (ε, U)

Computes the Floquet modes of one manifold driven by $H(t) = H_0 + H_1 e^{iΩt} + H_1^† e^{−iΩt}$, truncating the Floquet Hamiltonian at harmonic_cutoff harmonics.

For each bare level α, the returned mode is the Floquet eigenstate with maximum harmonic-0 weight on $|α⟩$ (unambiguous for weak dressing); ε[α] is its quasienergy (in angular frequency units, like H0), and U[:, it, α] the periodic part $u_α(t)$ sampled on num_time_samples uniform times over one drive period.

A warning is emitted whenever that maximum weight falls below min_overlap (the identification of the dressed state is then ambiguous, e.g. for a near-resonant drive).

Levels.PeriodicDynamics.sideband_rabi — Method
sideband_rabi(d::DressedTransition; sideband::Int, modulation = none)

Computes the first-order laser coupling between the dressed probed states at harmonic sideband of the drive frequency (+1: probing at ν + Ω, -1: ν − Ω, 0: carrier), with the laser phase-modulated by modulation (a HarmonicPhaseModulation, or any callable mapping the unitful time to the phase in radians).

Returns a NamedTuple with the effective Rabi frequency Ω = 2|g_n|, the resonance detuning δ_res (including the ac shifts of the dressed levels), the complex amplitude amp = g_n (for interference diagnostics), and the carrier Rabi frequency carrier.

Quasienergy shifts

Levels.PeriodicDynamics.ac_zeeman_shift — Function
ac_zeeman_shift(s::Eigenstructure, state, drive_frequency, b, method = PerturbationTheory())
ac_zeeman_shift(s::Eigenstructure, lower::StateSpec => upper::StateSpec,
                drive_frequency, b, method = PerturbationTheory())

Returns the ac Zeeman shift under a magnetic field with Cartesian amplitude phasor b, $\vec{B}(t) = \mathrm{Re}[\vec{b} e^{-iΩt}]$, where drive_frequency is the angular frequency $Ω$. Real phasors describe linearly polarised fields; complex phasors also describe elliptical fields.

Constructs the magnetic HarmonicDrives with zeeman_drives(s, b) and forwards to quasienergy_shift, with the same method choice and manifold requirements. state is a StateSpec or an index into basis(s). The state form returns an angular frequency; the pair form returns (; shift, lower, upper) with shift = upper - lower.

source

quasienergy_shift(dt::DrivenTransition, i::Integer, method = PerturbationTheory())

Returns the quasienergy shift of basis state i of the driven-transition model from its harmonic drives, over the manifold containing i (cf. the manifold blocks used by dress).

source
Levels.PeriodicDynamics.quasienergy_shift — Function
quasienergy_shift(s::Eigenstructure, state, drive_frequency, drives, method = PerturbationTheory())

Returns the quasienergy shift of the labelled eigenstate state (a StateSpec, or an index into basis(s)) of the structure s — which fixes the species, the static field and the basis — for driving at drive_frequency (angular units), as an angular frequency. The default method uses second-order perturbation theory; Floquet() performs nonperturbative dressing.

drives is a vector of HarmonicDrives at the same drive_frequency, over basis(s) in the structure's field eigenbasis. Every drive must be block-diagonal in the fine-structure manifolds; couplings between different $F$ levels of one manifold are allowed. The state's whole fine-structure manifold must be present and contiguous in the basis (cf. Levels.iscomplete). The plain-matrix form supports models with couplings between manifolds when the caller supplies their full static energies and drive matrix.

Magnetic drives may be constructed with zeeman_drives(s, b); ac_zeeman_shift accepts the field phasor directly. Works for both species kinds: on the exact hyperfine + Zeeman eigenstates of a hyperfine species, on the Zeeman-shifted $|J, m_J⟩$ states of a fine-structure one.

source
Levels.PeriodicDynamics.quasienergy_shift — Function
quasienergy_shift(s::Eigenstructure, lower::StateSpec => upper::StateSpec,
                drive_frequency, drives, method = PerturbationTheory())

Returns the differential quasienergy shift of the transition between the two labelled eigenstates of the structure s at its static field, for driving at drive_frequency (angular units), as a named tuple (; shift, lower, upper) with shift = upper - lower (positive: the transition frequency increases).

drives is a vector of HarmonicDrives over basis(s), at the same drive_frequency and block-diagonal in the fine-structure manifolds. The two probed states may lie in the same fine-structure manifold (e.g. a microwave qubit) or in two different ones; their manifolds must be complete and contiguous in s.

source
Levels.PeriodicDynamics.quasienergy_shift — Function
quasienergy_shift(H0::Diagonal, H1::AbstractMatrix, drive_frequency, α::Integer,
                method = PerturbationTheory())

Returns the quasienergy shift relative to the static energy of state α driven by $H(t) = H_0 + H_1 e^{iΩt} + H_1^† e^{−iΩt}$ (the convention of dress_manifold), from second-order Floquet perturbation theory:

\[δ_α = \sum_{k ≠ α} \frac{|H_{1,kα}|^2}{E_α - E_k - Ω} + \frac{|H^†_{1,kα}|^2}{E_α - E_k + Ω}.\]

All quantities are in angular frequency units. A warning is emitted whenever a denominator is smaller than method.warn_ratio times the corresponding coupling matrix element (near-resonant drive, where the perturbative treatment degrades — cross-check such cases with quasienergy_shift(..., Floquet())).

source
Levels.PeriodicDynamics.quasienergy_shift — Method
quasienergy_shift(H0::Diagonal, H1::AbstractMatrix, drive_frequency, α::Integer, method::Floquet)

Returns the quasienergy shift of state α from the nonperturbative Floquet dressing (dress_manifold, truncated at harmonic_cutoff harmonics): the difference between the dressed quasienergy and the static eigen-energy.

Exact in the drive amplitude up to the harmonic truncation, so it serves as the cross-check for quasienergy_shift near resonances; a warning is emitted when the dressed-state identification becomes ambiguous (harmonic-0 overlap below method.min_overlap).

Levels.PeriodicDynamics.monodromy — Function
monodromy(dt::DrivenTransition, method = Monodromy(); δ = 0, modulation = none)

Computes the one-drive-period propagator of $i \dot ψ = H(t)ψ$ at laser detuning δ from the probed component and the given phase modulation. method supplies the ODE algorithm and tolerances. Returns a dimensionless complex matrix over the model's basis.

source
Levels.PeriodicDynamics.probe_gap — Method

Wrapped quasienergy gap (plain angular $s^{-1}$) between the two Floquet states carrying the probed lower- and upper-state characters. At an isolated sideband resonance, the minimum gap over detuning gives the effective Rabi frequency.

source
Levels.PeriodicDynamics.sideband_rabi — Method
sideband_rabi(dt::DrivenTransition, method::Monodromy; sideband,
              modulation = none)

Returns (; Ω, δ_res) by minimising the Floquet quasienergy gap over laser detuning near an isolated sideband resonance. The search is centred on the prediction from method.estimate, a DressedStates configuration, and its width is set by method.bracket_scale.

One-period TDSE propagation includes the laser coupling nonperturbatively, including its light shifts. method.alg, method.reltol and method.abstol control propagation accuracy; the detuning search also has finite tolerance. This provides a slower cross-check of the first-order dressed-state result.

source
Levels.PeriodicDynamics.stripped_hamiltonian — Method

Returns a fast unit-stripped in-place evaluator ham!(H, t) of the full rotating-frame Hamiltonian of the model — the single point where the exact engine leaves unitful quantities (angular $\mathrm{rad}\,s^{-1}$, time in seconds), directly ahead of the ODE solves.

Levels.PeriodicDynamics.stroboscopic_populations — Function
stroboscopic_populations(dt::DrivenTransition, num_periods::Int;
                         δ = 0, modulation = none, initial = dt.lower)

Propagates the full model from the basis state with index initial (defaulting to the probed lower state) and returns the populations of all basis states at the stroboscopic times $k T$ for $k = 0 …$ num_periods, as a length(basis) × (num_periods + 1) matrix — a direct TDSE Rabi flop for validation. Rows follow the basis order (cf. Levels.stateindex).
