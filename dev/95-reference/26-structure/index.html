Level structure in a static field

An Eigenstructure solves the static hyperfine + Zeeman problem for a species and a selected basis. The accessors below expose its energies, eigenvectors and operators. States, fields and the structure explains their index spaces, projections and construction of manual operators.

Levels.Eigenstructure — Type

The solved hyperfine + Zeeman structure of a species at a static magnetic flux density along ẑ.

Constructed with Eigenstructure(species, basis; B). Each fine-structure manifold touched by the basis is solved in full; the basis selects and orders the labelled eigenstates used by operator matrices. For a hyperfine species these are field eigenstates labelled by their zero-field $F$ branch and conserved $m_F$; for a fine-structure species they are $|J, m_J⟩$ states with linear Zeeman energies.

offsets gives their centroid-relative angular energies, eigenvectors their coupled-basis components, and to_eigenbasis transforms coupled-basis operators. See Conventions for labels, phases and partial bases. Treat the structure and its stored arrays as read-only; accessors return them directly.

Levels.Eigenstructure — Method
Eigenstructure(species, basis::StateBasis; B)
Eigenstructure(species, levels...; B)
Eigenstructure(species, states::AbstractVector{<:StateSpec}; B)

Solves the level structure of species at the static flux density B along the quantisation axis ẑ (a signed scalar, e.g. 0.5u"mT"; 0u"mT" for the zero-field structure — there is deliberately no default) for the states of basis. The level form expands each level into all its states via StateBasis(species, levels...) (a fine-structure level of a hyperfine species into all its $F$ levels); the state form fixes an explicit subset and order.

The basis kind must match the species ($F$ levels for a hyperfine species, $J$ levels for a fine-structure one), every basis level must be a level of the species, and — for a hyperfine species — every basis manifold must carry hyperfine constants, since without them its $F$ levels cannot be located. Any subset of states is admitted: the eigenproblem is solved on the complete manifolds regardless, so partial bases address exact eigenstates (cf. iscomplete for the consumers that need whole manifolds).

source
Levels.ManifoldSolution — Type

One fine-structure manifold solved at a static field along ẑ: its canonical complete basis ($F$ ascending, $m$ ascending within each level, or the $|J, m_J⟩$ states of a fine-structure species), the eigen-energies relative to the manifold centroid (angular frequencies, aligned to basis) and the eigenvector columns in the coupled basis (aligned to basis). For a fine-structure species the eigenvectors are the identity and the energies the first-order Zeeman shifts.

Built by Levels.solve_manifold; the solutions of all manifolds of an Eigenstructure are available through Levels.manifold_solution.

Levels.basis(s::Eigenstructure)

Returns the selected StateBasis of the structure: the eigenstates every operator matrix built on s is indexed by, in that order. Not exported, as QuantumToolbox exports a basis of its own; s.basis reads the same field.

Levels.coupled_basis — Method
coupled_basis(s::Eigenstructure)

Returns the complete canonical zero-field basis of the structure's manifolds — the index space of coupled_hamiltonian, of the rows of eigenvectors and of the input of to_eigenbasis: every manifold the basis touches, in order of first appearance, each with its $F$ levels ascending and $m$ ascending within each (the $|J, m_J⟩$ states of a fine-structure species).

source
Levels.coupled_hamiltonian — Method
coupled_hamiltonian(s::Eigenstructure)

Returns the static offset Hamiltonian $H/ħ$ over coupled_basis(s), in angular-frequency units. Each fine-structure block contains the Casimir hyperfine shifts plus field(s) times the magnetic operator Levels.coupled_moments(species(s), level).z.

Each manifold's centroid is subtracted, giving to_eigenbasis(s, coupled_hamiltonian(s)) ≈ Diagonal(offsets(s)). state_energy adds the centroid; transition_frequency forms intervals between labelled eigenstates.

source
Levels.eigenvectors — Method
eigenvectors(s::Eigenstructure)

Returns the real eigenvector matrix $W$ of the structure: column k holds the coupled-basis components (rows indexed by coupled_basis) of the eigenstate labelled basis(s)[k]. The columns are orthonormal and supported within their own manifold; for a complete basis in canonical order $W$ is square and orthogonal, for a reordered or partial basis a column permutation or subset of it. A coupled-basis operator rotates into the eigenbasis as $W^† X W$ (to_eigenbasis).

For a fine-structure species $W$ is a column selection of the identity. At zero field it is the same for any species. The column signs follow the own-label-positive convention of Levels.solve_manifold: a whole-column sign change is a gauge choice that leaves every consistently computed observable unchanged.

field(s::Eigenstructure)

Returns the static flux density along ẑ the structure was solved at, in mT.

Levels.iscomplete — Method
iscomplete(s::Eigenstructure, level)

Returns whether basis(s) contains every state of the fine-structure manifold enclosing level, which may name a single $F$ level. Returns false for an absent manifold. Consumers requiring complete manifolds include Levels.PeriodicDynamics.DrivenTransition and Levels.PeriodicDynamics.quasienergy_shift.

source
Levels.manifold_hamiltonian_block — Method

Returns the hyperfine + Zeeman Hamiltonian block of one fine-structure manifold at the flux density B along ẑ over its canonical coupled basis, in angular frequency units, with the manifold centroid as the energy zero: the Casimir shifts on the diagonal (zero for a fine-structure species) plus B times the ẑ component of the magnetic moment.

Levels.manifold_solution — Method
Levels.manifold_solution(s::Eigenstructure, level)

Returns the Levels.ManifoldSolution of the fine-structure manifold enclosing level: the stored one for a manifold of the structure, a fresh solve at field(s) otherwise (which, for a hyperfine species, requires the level's hyperfine constants).

Levels.offsets — Method
offsets(s::Eigenstructure)

Returns the eigen-energy of every basis state relative to the zero-field centroid of its fine-structure level, as angular frequencies aligned to basis(s) (cf. state_offset).

Levels.resolve_state — Method

Resolves a state label to the manifold solution it belongs to and its index in that manifold's canonical basis, accepting any state of coupled_basis(s) (not only basis states). Raises for a state of the wrong kind, of a manifold the structure does not contain, or with an invalid projection.

source
Levels.solve_manifold — Method
solve_manifold(species, fs_level, B; require_constants = true) -> ManifoldSolution

Solves one complete fine-structure manifold at the static flux density B along ẑ, returning centroid-relative angular energies and coupled-basis eigenvectors.

For a fine-structure species the eigenvectors are the identity and the energies are linear Zeeman shifts. For a hyperfine species the hyperfine + Zeeman Hamiltonian is diagonalised separately in each conserved-$m_F$ block. Within a block, $F$ labels follow the energy order of the zero-field levels. At B = 0 the coupled states and their Casimir energies are returned directly. Ties between zero-field levels are ordered by ascending $F$; these labels have no unique adiabatic continuation.

Each eigenvector's component on its own zero-field label is made positive. If it is exactly zero, the largest-magnitude component is made positive. This gives the identity limit for a resolved zero-field spectrum, but permits sign jumps at finite field; see Conventions.

Missing hyperfine constants raise an error. With require_constants = false, that manifold is instead returned degenerate at its centroid, with identity eigenvectors and no Zeeman shift. This approximation is used only for far-detuned polarisability intermediates.

Levels.state_energy — Method
state_energy(s::Eigenstructure, state)
state_energy(s::Eigenstructure, level, m)

Returns the energy of the labelled eigenstate in joules, relative to the species' common energy reference: the centroid energy of its fine-structure level (level_energy) plus $ħ$ times state_offset. Subtracting total energies to form a small splitting can lose precision; transition_frequency keeps centroid and offset terms separate.

source
Levels.state_offset — Method
state_offset(s::Eigenstructure, state)
state_offset(s::Eigenstructure, level, m)

Returns the eigen-energy of the labelled eigenstate relative to the zero-field centroid of its fine-structure level, in angular frequency units — for a hyperfine species the exact hyperfine + Zeeman eigen-energy of the adiabatically labelled state, for a fine-structure species the first-order Zeeman shift. Any state of coupled_basis(s) may be queried, not only basis states. state_energy adds the centroid, giving an absolute energy in joules.

source
Levels.to_eigenbasis — Method
to_eigenbasis(s::Eigenstructure, X)

Rotates the matrix X, given over the complete coupled basis coupled_basis(s), into the field eigenbasis of the structure: $W^† X W$ with $W$ = eigenvectors(s), indexed by basis(s). For a partial basis the result holds the exact matrix elements between the retained eigenstates; a matrix over basis(s) itself is refused, as the eigenstates have components on coupled states outside the subset, so no exact rotation exists from that input. Projection does not preserve products: for a partial basis $(W^† X W)(W^† Y W) ≠ W^† X Y W$ in general.

source
Levels.transition_frequency — Method
transition_frequency(s::Eigenstructure, lower::StateSpec => upper::StateSpec)

Returns the angular frequency of the transition between the two labelled eigenstates at the structure's field: the centroid interval of their fine-structure levels plus the difference of their state_offsets, formed with the two terms separated so that same-manifold (microwave) intervals and small differences keep their precision. A pair of levels forwards to the zero-field species form.

source
Levels.validate_field — Method

Returns the static flux density as a Float64 quantity in mT, raising an error unless it is a real, finite, scalar flux density (zero included).

source
Levels.zeeman_hamiltonian — Method
zeeman_hamiltonian(s::Eigenstructure, b)

Returns the Zeeman Hamiltonian of a magnetic field with the Cartesian 3-vector b (a perturbing field, e.g. the amplitude of an ac field — not the static field, which is fixed in s) in the field eigenbasis of the structure, indexed by basis(s): the coupled-basis matrix of zeeman_hamiltonian(species, basis, b) over coupled_basis(s) rotated with to_eigenbasis. Exact matrix elements between the retained eigenstates for any subset or order.

source
Levels.zeeman_sensitivity — Method
zeeman_sensitivity(s::Eigenstructure, lower::StateSpec => upper::StateSpec)

Returns the magnetic-field sensitivity $χ = (1/ħ) d(E_u - E_l)/dB$ of the transition between the two labelled eigenstates at the structure's field, in angular frequency units per flux density. Exact (Hellmann–Feynman theorem on the eigenstates, no numerical differentiation), so valid in the Breit–Rabi regime of a hyperfine species; the transition may lie within one fine-structure manifold or connect two. For a fine-structure species it is the field-independent $(g_u m_u - g_l m_l) μ_B/ħ$.

source
Levels.zeeman_shift — Method
zeeman_shift(s::Eigenstructure, state)

Returns the Zeeman shift $[E(B) - E(0)]/ħ$ of the labelled eigenstate at the structure's field, in angular frequency units: state_offset minus the zero-field hyperfine shift of the state's level. Exact for both species kinds (for a hyperfine species nonlinear in the field in the Breit–Rabi regime); zero at B = 0.
